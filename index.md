# opengwasr

[![R-CMD-check](https://github.com/opengwas/opengwasr/actions/workflows/R-CMD-check.yaml/badge.svg?branch=main)](https://github.com/opengwas/opengwasr/actions/workflows/R-CMD-check.yaml)

The R client for OpenGWAS data. It queries Analyses, Associations, top
hits and LD either through the OpenGWAS API or from Store Releases
downloaded to your own machine, and replaces
[ieugwasr](https://github.com/MRCIEU/ieugwasr) and
[gwasvcf](https://github.com/MRCIEU/gwasvcf) as the data-access layer
for [TwoSampleMR](https://github.com/MRCIEU/TwoSampleMR).

> **Status: design.** In development: the package skeleton and its
> checks are in place, and no query functions exist yet. The vocabulary
> is in [GLOSSARY.md](https://opengwas.github.io/opengwasr/GLOSSARY.md)
> and the decisions so far are in
> [docs/adr/](https://opengwas.github.io/opengwasr/docs/adr/).
